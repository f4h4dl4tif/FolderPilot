FolderPilot is installed

Opening it the first time: FolderPilot isn't from the App Store, so macOS may say it can't verify the developer. If so, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to FolderPilot. You only need to do this once.

Getting started:

  • FolderPilot lives in the menu bar (folder icon) and keeps working when its window is closed.
  • The starter rules are switched off. Use Preview & Run to see exactly what a rule would do before anything moves.
  • Every change is recorded in Activity and can be undone.
  • macOS asks once for access to each folder you watch.
