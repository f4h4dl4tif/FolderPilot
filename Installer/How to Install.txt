How to install FolderPilot
==========================

1. Drag FolderPilot onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open FolderPilot from Applications. macOS will say it "could not verify
   FolderPilot is free of malware". This happens because FolderPilot is a free,
   open-source app that isn't notarized by Apple. Click "Done" (don't move it
   to the Bin).

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security
   section, and click "Open Anyway" next to FolderPilot. Enter your password
   and click "Open Anyway" again. You only need to do this once.

   Alternative (Terminal), after copying FolderPilot to Applications:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FolderPilot.app

4. FolderPilot lives in your menu bar (folder icon) and opens its main window.
   The two starter rules are switched off: review one, turn it on, or click
   "Preview & Run" to see what it would do before anything moves.
   macOS asks once for access to each folder you watch (Downloads, Desktop…).

Optional: turn on "Launch FolderPilot at login" in FolderPilot > Settings.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
Free and open source (MIT License).
