FolderPilot

FolderPilot watches the folders you choose and organizes new files automatically with simple rules: rename, move, copy, tag or trash them based on their name, kind, size, dates or even the text inside them.

Example: every time an invoice PDF lands in Downloads, read its date, rename it, tag it “Finance” and file it under Finance/2026/03.

This installer puts FolderPilot in your Applications folder.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Free and open source (MIT License).
